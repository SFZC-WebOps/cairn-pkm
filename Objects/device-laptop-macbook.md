Primary laptop for work and personal use. M3 Pro MacBook Pro 14" with 18GB RAM and 512GB SSD. Purchased to replace aging 2018 Intel MacBook Pro.

## Usage
**Primary Use Cases:**
- Software development (VS Code, Docker)
- Web browsing and email
- Video calls (Zoom, Google Meet)
- Document editing
- Media consumption

**Performance:**
Excellent. M3 Pro handles everything smoothly. Battery life is outstanding - typically 8-10 hours of mixed use. The display is fantastic for photo editing and development work.

**Mobility:**
- Primary location: Home office desk (new setup)
- Portable when needed for coffee shop work or travel
- Power adapter kept at desk, use MagSafe
- Backup USB-C charger in travel bag

**Updates:**

**Issues:**

## Notes
This was a significant upgrade from the 2018 Intel model. The performance difference is night and day - compiles are faster, battery lasts longer, and it runs cool and quiet.

The Space Black finish is beautiful but shows fingerprints easily. Not a functional concern, just aesthetic.

AppleCare+ was worth it for peace of mind. Previous laptop had a keyboard issue that was covered under warranty.

**Integration with Home Office:**
- Connected to dual monitors via USB-C hub
- MagSafe charging from left side
- Clamshell mode when docked
- Open lid mode when in laptop-only setup

**Future Considerations:**
- This should last 4-5 years minimum
- May upgrade to 32GB RAM model if doing more intensive work
- Storage is adequate but could expand with external drives if needed
