# Standing desk productivity benefits - research study

**Source:** https://journals.lww.com/joem/abstract/2018/03000/stand_up_to_work__assessing_the_health_impacts_of.8.aspx
**Captured:** 2026-06-22 14:30

## Description
Research study on health impacts of standing workstations. Found significant reduction in upper back and neck pain among office workers who used standing desks. Study followed 146 participants over 6 months.

Key findings:
- 54% reduction in upper back/neck pain
- Improved mood states
- No impact (positive or negative) on productivity
- Most effective when alternating sit/stand throughout day

## Why Captured
Researching standing desk benefits for home office project. Good evidence for the investment. Study suggests alternating is key - supports plan to get desk with presets.

## Notes
Sample size is decent (146 participants). Study is from peer-reviewed journal. Confirms that standing desk should have easy height adjustments for frequent position changes.

Reinforces decision to get motorized desk with memory presets rather than manual crank model.
