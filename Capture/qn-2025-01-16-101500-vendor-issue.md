# Quick Note: Office Supply Vendor Issue

Created: 2025-01-16 10:15:00

## Context

Called [[vendor-acme-office-supplies|Acme Office Supplies]] about delayed order. Need to decide if we should switch vendors.

## Content

**What happened:**
- Paper order placed Jan 10, promised delivery Jan 13
- Still not delivered as of Jan 16
- Called customer service - order was "lost in system"
- Now saying delivery will be Jan 22 (9 days late)

**Impact:**
- Low on printer paper in multiple departments
- Had to do emergency run to local store ($150 at retail prices vs $85 bulk)
- Third delay from Acme in past 6 months

**Options:**
1. Stick with Acme - cheapest pricing, but unreliable
2. Switch to Better Business Supplies - slightly higher cost, good reviews
3. Split orders between two vendors - redundancy but more complex

**Next steps:**
- Check contract with Acme - any minimum commitment?
- Get pricing from Better Business for comparison
- Talk to other companies about their supply vendors
- Make decision by end of month
