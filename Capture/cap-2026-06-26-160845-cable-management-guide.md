# Best practices for cable management in standing desk setups

**Source:** https://www.wirecutter.com/blog/how-to-manage-your-cables/
**Captured:** 2026-06-26 16:08

## Description
Wirecutter's comprehensive guide to cable management. Covers under-desk trays, cable sleeves, velcro ties, and best practices for routing cables on adjustable-height desks.

Key recommendations:
- Leave extra slack for standing position (critical!)
- Use cable tray with smooth edges (prevents cable damage)
- Velcro ties over zip ties (reusable, adjustable)
- Label everything for future changes
- Route power separate from data cables when possible

## Why Captured
Cable tray just got backordered - good time to research proper installation before it arrives. Want to do this right the first time rather than redo it.

## Notes
**Important insight:** Need minimum 8" of slack in cables to allow full range of desk height adjustment. Didn't think about this before - could have caused issues.

**Product recommendations from article:**
- J Channel cable tray (what I ordered - good choice)
- Velcro ties in multiple sizes
- Cable labels with write-on surface

**Process they recommend:**
1. Install tray first
2. Route power cables
3. Add data/monitor cables
4. Bundle with velcro
5. Label everything
6. Test full range of desk motion
