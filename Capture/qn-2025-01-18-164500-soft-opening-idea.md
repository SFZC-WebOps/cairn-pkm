# Quick Note: New Office Move Idea

Created: 2025-01-18 16:45:00

## Context

Conversation with [[contact-sarah-chen|Sarah Chen (CEO)]] about the office move. She suggested something I hadn't considered.

## Content

**Sarah's idea:**
"What if we do a soft opening week before the official move? Let a few people work from the new space, test everything, find problems while we still have the old office as backup."

**Why this is smart:**
- Catch IT issues before everyone moves
- Test furniture setup with real people
- Identify missing items (trash cans, coat racks, etc.)
- Less stressful than "hard cutover"

**Details to work out:**
- Which team volunteers for soft opening?
- How do we handle phones/mail during transition?
- Insurance implications of operating two locations?
- Does this change the moving company timeline?

**My thoughts:**
This could prevent a lot of chaos. Marketing team might be good volunteers - they're flexible and tech-savvy.

Need to:
- Run this by IT director
- Check with moving company
- Update timeline if we do this
