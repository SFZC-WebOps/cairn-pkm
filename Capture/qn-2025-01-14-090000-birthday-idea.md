# Quick Note: Birthday Calendar Reminder

Created: 2025-01-14 09:00:00

## Context

[[contact-patricia-anderson|Patricia]] mentioned we used to celebrate employee birthdays but stopped during COVID. Should we restart?

## Content

**The idea:**
Bring back monthly birthday celebrations - simple cake in break room, no gifts or big production.

**Why people liked it:**
- Nice morale boost
- Chance for team to socialize
- Shows company cares about people
- Fun break in the workday

**Logistics to consider:**
- Who maintains the birthday list?
- How do we handle remote employees?
- Dietary restrictions (need dairy-free, gluten-free options)
- What if someone doesn't want their birthday celebrated?
- Budget? (~$30/month for cake)

**Questions:**
- Do people still want this, or is it outdated?
- Should we ask via quick survey?
- Do other offices do this anymore?

**My take:**
Small gesture, low cost, potentially good for culture. Worth trying for a few months?
